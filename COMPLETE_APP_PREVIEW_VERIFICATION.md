# Complete App Preview Verification

The HomeOS project preview was validated at a 468 × 828 mobile viewport. The visible application now contains customer onboarding, Fix Anything intake, guided assessment, controlled matching, tracking, quote approval, OTP-gated completion, checkout, invoice, 30-day warranty, jobs, Home Service Passport, account settings, technician job handling, proof/OTP controls, and an operations console.

All of the following direct preview states rendered successfully: `home`, `onboarding`, `fix`, `assessment`, `matching`, `tracking`, `quote`, `otp`, `checkout`, `invoice`, `jobs`, `passport`, `account`, `technician`, `techJob`, `techQuote`, `techProof`, and `operations`. The final production build completed successfully after the interaction work.

The interactive proof-added and completion-OTP-requested technician states were also rendered and reviewed. Each operations action panel—technician verification, pricing review, live job monitoring, and service-health analytics—was directly rendered and reviewed at mobile width.

The application remains explicit about production integration boundaries: live mobile identity, real-time device GPS, push delivery, and payment-provider activation need their respective production configuration before processing real customers. The visible product experience and the protected backend workflow contracts are implemented in the project.
