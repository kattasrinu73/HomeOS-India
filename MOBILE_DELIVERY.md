# Mobile Delivery Direction

HomeOS India will be delivered as a native-feeling mobile application for customer and technician workflows on iOS and Android. The existing full-stack project defines the service domain, data model, and backend integration contract. A dedicated Expo application will be initialized as the primary product surface, while the browser interface is reserved for internal operations and administration.

The customer and technician experiences share the same domain model but present role-specific navigation, actions, and safeguards. The mobile interface will support onboarding, service requests, AI diagnosis, controlled dispatch, itemised quotes, OTP-gated completion, payment, invoices, 30-day warranties, and the Home Service Passport.
