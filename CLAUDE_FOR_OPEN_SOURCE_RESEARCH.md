# Claude for Open Source Research Notes

**Research date:** 27 August 2026

## What the program is

Anthropic’s **Claude for Open Source Program** offers selected eligible individuals a complimentary six-month Claude Max 20x subscription. It is not the ordinary Claude Code free tier: Claude Code is listed as a feature of paid plans, while this program is a separate competitive benefit for qualifying open-source maintainers and contributors.[1][2]

## Eligibility that repository presentation cannot replace

An applicant must be a natural person of eligible age and geography, have a GitHub account in good standing that is at least two years old, have public open-source activity within the preceding 90 days, and maintain or contribute to a project with an OSI-approved license.[1]

The applicant must also meet at least one maintainer-track threshold or make a credible ecosystem-impact case. The published thresholds include 500 dependent repositories, 100 dependent packages, 200,000 monthly package downloads, 100 merged pull requests to repositories the applicant does not own in the last 12 months, 20 unique external contributors with merged pull requests in the last 12 months, or an OpenSSF criticality score of at least 0.4.[1]

> **Integrity boundary:** Anthropic says it evaluates the substance of underlying activity and may discount trivial, duplicative, automated, or otherwise inauthentic activity. It reserves the right to disqualify applicants who inflate eligibility metrics or provide misleading information.[1]

## What a reviewer-ready repository can demonstrate

Repository improvements cannot create eligibility metrics. They can, however, make real work legible by providing an OSI-approved license, a clear README, reproducible setup and validation steps, accurate architecture and security boundaries, contribution guidance, and honest evidence links. The application itself asks for a brief intended-use statement and an explanation of qualification of no more than 500 words.[1]
