# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want HomeOS India to be a respectful and practical place for users, maintainers, and contributors. Participation should be free from harassment, intimidation, discrimination, and personal attacks.

## Expected behaviour

Contributors should communicate constructively, respect differing technical views, protect user privacy, and focus discussion on the project. Feedback is most useful when it is specific, reproducible, and considerate.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, hate speech, threats, doxxing, sharing private information, deliberate disruption, and abusive conduct are not acceptable. Do not publish credentials, home addresses, documents, OTPs, payment data, or other personal information.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Security and privacy vulnerabilities should follow the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may limit participation when needed to protect the community and project users.
